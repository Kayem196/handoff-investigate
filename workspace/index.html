Choose a group to investigate

The picker reads up to 200 visible groups, 10 pages and 50 SDK requests, with a 120-second deadline and 4 MiB response limit. It does not read membership, owners, activity or per-group policy counts.

Collect fresh policy evidence?

This resolves the exact group and reads visible Conditional Access policies, Intune device-compliance policies and their assignments. It also reads the names of other groups and assignment filters those policies reference (up to 50 and 25). It does not evaluate membership or predict who is affected.

Limits: 120 seconds, 100 pages per collection, 10,000 retained items, 1,000 SDK requests, 4 MiB per response. Intune assignments use the previously verified beta endpoint to retain filter metadata.

A new immutable observation is saved locally. Earlier evidence and chats stay available. AI does not receive the new evidence automatically; the new observation needs its own sharing approval.
